Teaching

Teaching, for me, is an extension of research: both are driven by a commitment to making complex dynamics legible.

My teaching philosophy rests on two beliefs: that the most common barriers to learning are emotional rather than cognitive, and that effective learning requires close alignment between in-class activities, assignments, and learning objectives. I design each course backward from clear learning objectives, and I work deliberately to build the sense of belonging that lets students take intellectual risks.

These convictions emerged from my own path from a small liberal arts college to a Ph.D. program, and they were sharpened by professional training at UC San Diego — a 20-week sequence in college instruction and a 10-week Evidence-Based Teaching Workshop. My commitment to this approach has been recognized with the TA Excellence Award (2023–24), selection as a Summer Graduate Teaching Scholar (2025), and appointments as instructor of record at UC San Diego and MacEwan University.
